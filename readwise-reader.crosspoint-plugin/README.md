Readwise Reader

Downloads the EPUB books in your Readwise Reader library to /Readwise
on the SD card.

Setup (once):
1. Get your access token at readwise.io/access_token.
2. Turn on the reader's web page (File Transfer) and open it in a
   browser on your phone or computer.
3. Go to Settings, find the Readwise Reader card, paste the token and
   tap Connect.

Use: on the reader, open Home > Plugins > Readwise Reader, pick a list
(Inbox, Later, Shortlist, Archive or All EPUBs) and choose a book to
download it.

Limits:
- Only EPUBs you uploaded to Reader. Articles, PDFs and other document
  types are not listed.
- Each list shows up to 100 books.
- Books Readwise cannot share as a file (for example Wisereads
  previews) fail to download.

Privacy: the token is stored unencrypted in config.json in this
plugin's folder. Disconnect on the Readwise Reader card removes it.
You can also revoke the token on readwise.io.
